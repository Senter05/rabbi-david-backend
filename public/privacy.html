Your privacy, in plain language

Private local preview. This site is not currently open to customers.

Why we ask for your name and email

Your first name and email are required before the first test question. They connect you to your reading and its delivery. No password is needed. The email format is checked; ownership verification is not connected in this preview.

Your answers and personalization

Your answers, reading level, activity progress and email preferences are saved in a local database. A necessary browser cookie connects this browser to its reading.

When you request an AI-assisted reading or follow-up question, the relevant answers are sent through OpenRouter to the configured text model. For audio, the narration text is sent to ai33.pro. Your email address is not included in the text-generation prompt. Avoid including account numbers or private information about other people.

Delivery and optional follow-ups

Saving an email for your reading does not automatically subscribe you to marketing. The follow-ups after 24 hours and 5 days are optional. You can change that preference in your reading space.

All emails stay in the local test inbox. This preview does not send external email, use advertising trackers or share a contact list with Meta.

Before public launch

The final service must identify the responsible business, establish retention and deletion procedures, connect the email provider and publish its complete privacy policy. This page describes the local test version.
