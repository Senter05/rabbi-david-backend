✦ Clarity Before You Begin

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about the test, your reading, the book library and how this preview works. Take a moment to explore whatever matters to you before you begin.

How does the abundance test work? +

Start with your name and email. Then answer 10–15 questions about your priorities, preferences and daily life. Your answers shape a personal reading with ideas for reflection. You can take your time; there is no right answer to aim for.

Do I need to be Jewish or know the Torah? +

No prior knowledge is needed. The experience draws on Jewish wisdom and welcomes people from different backgrounds. Consider the ideas in light of your own beliefs and circumstances.

What is free, and what do the paid options include? +

The opening 40% of your reading is free, with no card required. The complete written reading is $7. The $32 option adds a fourteen-day plan shaped around your answers and an audio reading based on your own priorities. If you already chose the $7 reading, the upgrade is $25. Payments are not enabled in this preview.

What do you mean by abundance? +

Abundance can mean more than money: a calmer start to the day, purpose in your work, closer relationships or values you want to pass on. The test asks what matters to you rather than assuming everyone wants the same thing.

Can I keep my answers if I choose the personal plan later? +

Yes. Your reading and answers stay together. Moving from the complete written reading to the $32 option uses the same answers, so you do not need to repeat the test.

What makes the fourteen-day plan personal to me? +

Each day includes an action and a reflection based on the priorities, preferences and available time you shared. The aim is a manageable step that fits your life. It is prepared from your answers, rather than taken from a single plan given to every reader.

What will I hear in the audio reading? +

The audio follows your written reading and reflects the priorities you shared. It uses the Rabbi David channel voice through voice synthesis. You can listen in your reading space when it is ready; it is not a recording of an individual consultation.

Are any books included with my reading? +

Books are sold separately. Your reading may recommend one that connects with your interests, but buying a book is optional. You do not need one to follow the fourteen-day plan.

Can I read the books on my phone? +

The books are digital e-books. You can open an e-book on a phone, tablet or computer with a suitable reader and zoom in when needed. Each book page lists its language and page count. Live book purchase and delivery are not connected in this preview.

How do I choose between the books? +

Choose Generational Wealth: The Torah Method ($77) for family values and legacy, or The 7 Jewish Money Rituals ($32) for symbolic everyday practices. The Complete Rabbi’s Wealth System ($150) brings four complete ebooks together, including both individual titles, Morning Wealth Blessing and the Complete Wealth System ebook. Morning Wealth Blessing is only available in the bundle.

How are my answers and email used? +

Your name and email connect you to your reading. Your answers are used to prepare it, with AI assistance through OpenRouter and, for audio, ai33.pro. Optional follow-up emails require your choice. The privacy page explains what is stored and what is sent to these services. Transactional email delivery is active; reminders require your consent.

Does the reading promise money or financial results? +

No. It offers spiritual reflection and practical prompts, not a prediction, financial advice or a promise of wealth. You can begin the free preview without buying anything and decide whether the approach is useful to you.

What is the refund policy for tests and e-books? +

Personal test purchases (including the $7 complete reading, 14-day plan, and audio narration) are single, one-time payments and are strictly non-refundable due to the immediate digital generation and personalized nature of the content. E-books from our library are the only products eligible for refunds (within 14 days of purchase).

How can I return to my reading? +

Use My Reading on this browser to return to your saved session. If you need an access link, open Help and enter the same email you used for the test. A secure single-use access link is sent to your email. You can also use your private recovery key.
