Cookies are small browser records. Rabbi David uses a necessary cookie to associate this browser with a private saved reading. Static pages do not need a reading session; opening interactive features may establish one.

3. OPTIONAL TRACKING

This version does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or session replay. No “accept all” choice is needed to enable tracking that is not installed. If optional tracking is introduced, this notice and the corresponding controls will be updated before it is activated.

4. YOUR CONTROLS

You can block or remove cookies in your browser settings, or sign out from the Help page. Blocking the session cookie prevents saved access from working. Save your PDF or private recovery key before clearing browser storage. Removing a browser cookie does not itself delete the saved record on the server.
